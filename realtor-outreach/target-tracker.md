# C&O TV Mounting — Realtor Referral Tracker (Outreach Pipeline)

Track each realtor you contact. Status flow:
`cold → contacted → follow-up → in ↔ paused` / `signed-up` (has referral code) / `referred (1+)`

Columns to log (Google Sheet or this):
- **Realtor Name, Brokerage/Firm, City, Phone, Email, Source (personal/FB/event/brokerage)**
- **Status** (cold/contacted/follow-up/in/signed-up/referred/paused)
- **Date First Contacted**
- **Reply?** (Y/N)
- **Referral Code Assigned** (from partner dashboard)
- **# Referrals Sent**
- **Controller** (last follow-up date)
- **Notes** (referred service, payout due)

## Lead-Gen Ideas for Realtors
- **Offer to photograph a clean mount in a vacant/listed home** — they get a testimonial photo, you get a demo.
- **Open-house drop**: leave your card + one-pager at open houses in luxury/growing neighborhoods.
- **Home-stagers** and **contractors** are your second best channel — they also go into homes.
- **Referral reciprocity**: offer to refer clients who need a realtor; realtors LOVE two-way referral streams.

## Weekly Rhythm (recommended)
- **Mon:** send 5-10 new cold contacts (email or DM)
- **Wed:** follow up on last week's no-replies
- **Fri:** thank-you texts to anyone who referred; log payouts
- Goal: hold ~15-20 active realtor partners → 3-7 referrals/week
