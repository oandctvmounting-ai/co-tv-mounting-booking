## Getting Started

### How do I become a referral partner?
Enter your email at the Partner Portal. If you're new, you'll create your profile (name, company, email) and receive your unique referral code instantly.

### Where is my referral code?
After logging in, your code is displayed in the top navigation bar and on the **My Card** tab. Format: `REF-ABC123`.

### How do I share my referral code?
- **Referral Card Link:** On the **My Card** tab, copy your personalized link (e.g., `.../referral-card.html?code=REF-ABC123`)
- **Email Templates:** Use the pre-written templates on the **Overview** tab
- **Direct:** Just give clients your code — they enter it at booking

## Referral Tracking

### How do I track my referrals?
The **Referrals** tab shows all submissions with status:
- **New** → **Contacted** → **Quoted** → **Booked** → **Installed** → **Paid**

### When does a referral count?
Only when the client:
1. Books using your code (or your referral link)
2. Completes the installation
3. Pays in full

### Can I see client details?
Yes — the Referrals table shows client name, phone, service, status, and date.

| Quarterly Bonus | 10+ | $250 |

### When do I get paid?
- **Per-job commissions:** Within 7 business days of job completion

Via Square Payment Link (email) or direct deposit. First payout requires W-9.

$50 minimum. Smaller amounts accumulate until threshold is reached.

## Troubleshooting

### "My referral isn't showing"
- Check the **Referrals** tab — filter by status
- Ensure client used your exact code at booking
- Allow 2 hours for new submissions to appear

### "Client says they used my code but no commission"
- Verify code matches exactly (case-insensitive)
- Confirm job was completed AND paid
- Contact us with client name/date — we'll investigate

### "Forgot my referral code"
It's in the top nav bar after login. Or check **My Card** tab.

### "Want to update my profile"
Log out and back in with your email — you can update name/company on re-entry.
