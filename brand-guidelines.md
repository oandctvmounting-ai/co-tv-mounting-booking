## Usage Rules

### Do:
- Use logo with minimum 24px clear space on all sides
- Use Royal Blue for primary actions and headers
- Use Gold for accents, highlights, and premium messaging
- Maintain aspect ratio — never stretch logo
- Use provided SVG files for best quality

### Don't:
- Change logo colors or proportions
- Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
- Place logo on busy/clashing backgrounds
- Use outdated logo versions
- Combine with other brands without approval
