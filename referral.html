Common Questions

When do I get paid? +
Payouts are sent monthly via Square or Venmo. You earn $25–$75 for each completed job from your referral. Bonuses ($100 for 5+ jobs/quarter, $200 for 10+) are paid quarterly.
Does my client know I referred them? +
That's up to you. Some partners introduce us directly. Others just send us the info and we contact the client on their behalf. Either way, we keep you in the loop.
What services count? +
All C&O services: TV mounting, electric fireplace installation, blinds & shades, mirrors & portraits, LED lighting, and soundbar mounting. Multiple services on one job still count as one referral payout.
Do I need to be a contractor? +
No. Anyone can be a referral partner — electricians, remodelers, interior designers, real estate agents, or just someone with a network. If you know people who need TV mounting, you can earn.
How do I track my referrals? +
Every referral gets a unique code (like REF-SMITH-ELEC-ABC). Use the "View My Referrals" button to see real-time status: New → Contacted → Quoted → Booked → Paid.
