# C&O TV Mounting — Referral Partner Agreement

**Effective Date:** August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Partnership Overview

C&O TV Mounting ("Company") welcomes you as a Referral Partner ("Partner"). This agreement outlines the terms for referring clients to C&O TV Mounting for TV mounting and related installation services in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

---

## 2. Referral Process

1. **Submission** — Partner submits referrals via the Partner Portal at `https://oandctvmounting-ai.github.io/co-tv-mounting-booking/referral.html` using their unique referral code.

2. **Tracking** — All referrals are tracked by the Partner's unique code. The referral code must be provided at time of booking for commission eligibility.

3. **Qualification** — A referral qualifies for commission when:
   - The client books and completes an installation
   - Payment is collected in full
   - The referral code was used at booking

---

## 3. Commission Structure

| Tier | Quarterly Installs | Bonus |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| Quarterly Bonus | 10+ | $250 |
| VIP Partner | 20+ | $500 |
| Elite | 40+ | $1,000 |

- **Per-job referral fee:** $25–$75 per completed job (based on job value)
- **Quarterly bonuses:** Paid within 15 business days of quarter end
- **Minimum payout:** $50 (accumulates until reached)

---

## 4. Payment Terms

- Commissions paid via Square Payment Link or direct deposit
- Partner must provide W-9 for tax purposes (1099-NEC issued for $600+/year)
- Disputes resolved within 30 days; Company's records are final

---

## 5. Partner Obligations

- Represent C&O TV Mounting professionally and accurately
- Use only approved branding assets (available in Partner Portal)
- Do not guarantee specific pricing, timelines, or outcomes
- Referrals must be genuine — no self-referrals or fraudulent submissions

---

## 6. Company Obligations

- Provide 24-month workmanship warranty on all installations
- Respond to referred clients within 2 business hours
- Maintain professional service standards
- Provide referral tracking and reporting via Partner Portal

---

## 7. Term & Termination

- Agreement continues until terminated by either party (30 days written notice)
- Company may terminate immediately for fraud, misrepresentation, or brand misuse
- Earned commissions paid through termination date
